03 Fit Proportion, considered

Your size.
Your length.
Separately.

Your shirt size shouldn’t decide your shirt length.

Your usual size feels tight. You go one size up, it fits better around your body, but now the shirt is too long.

That extra length can make your body look shorter and throw off the proportion.

NABZ separates size and length.

Choose your size for the fit around your body. Then choose the length.
