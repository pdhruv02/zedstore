# NABZ Background Lab

A controlled comparison of six continuous textile worlds for the NABZ landing page.

## Comparison rule

Every study uses the same full-scroll landing page, typography, spacing, real NABZ photography, section order, motion, fit interaction and responsive behavior. Only the background image and its contrast treatment change.

## Prototype content

- Custom NABZ navigation
- Editorial hero using Self Contour photography
- Proportion thesis
- Four-state embroidery surface gallery
- Real M Standard and M Extended fit interaction
- Air Cable editorial section
- Three-study collection gateway
- Quiet closing section

## Explicit exclusions

- No illustrated or generated garment
- No SVG shirt silhouette
- No CSS stretching to fake fit differences
- No substitute model photography
- No hard background shift between sections
- No long em dash or en dash characters in permanent site copy
