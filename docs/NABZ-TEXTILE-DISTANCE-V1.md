## Locked rules

- The live Shopify theme remains untouched.
- No long em dashes appear in homepage copy.
- Commerce routes remain standard Shopify routes.
- The loader is short and first-visit only.
- Mobile and reduced-motion states are included.
- Fit uses the approved S, M, L, XL and Standard or Extended measurement logic.
